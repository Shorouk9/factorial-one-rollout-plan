Rolling out Factorial One, market by market

Factorial rebuilt its platform around AI agents. Moving 17,000+ existing customers onto it is a programme, not a feature launch. This planner sizes each wave from real team capacity, puts a go/no-go gate between waves, and keeps one risk log for every market.

MarketCustomersWave

Why this order

  • Pilot and wave 1 at home. Spain is the largest and best-known base, with the support team in the same building.
  • Wave 2: similar EU markets. France, Italy and the UK reuse the playbook with new languages.
  • Wave 3: Germany on its own. Works councils, DATEV and the new Empion customers all need extra care.
  • Wave 4: LATAM and the Gulf. Different payroll rules and time zones; run them once the playbook is proven.

Customer counts are an illustrative split of about 17,000 customers; Germany (1,700+) and Italy (1,500+) are public figures. Change any number.
